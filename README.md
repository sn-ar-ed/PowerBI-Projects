# 📊 Power BI Dashboard Collection

## Overview

This repository contains a collection of interactive Power BI dashboards developed to analyze data across different business domains. Each dashboard focuses on solving real-world business problems using data visualization, KPIs, slicers, and interactive reports.

> **Note on data sources:** The Real Mart Sales, Pizza Order Analysis, and UK 
> Road Accidents dashboards use genuine publicly available datasets. The Movie 
> Dataset Analysis and AI Impact on Indian IT Sectors dashboards use 
> synthetic/practice data created to demonstrate Power BI skills — figures in 
> those two dashboards should be treated as illustrative rather than real 
> statistics.
---

## Dashboards Included

### 🎬 Movie Analysis Dashboard
- Analyzes movie ratings, genres, revenue, popularity, and release trends.
- Provides insights into top-performing movies and audience preferences.
![Movie Analysis Dashboard](movie%20analysis.png)
**Key finding:** A small number of movies drove a disproportionate share of total revenue, and popularity didn't always track with rating — some highly-rated titles had low popularity scores, suggesting exposure/marketing matters as much as quality.

### 🚗 UK Road Accidents Dashboard
- Examines accident patterns based on location, vehicle type, road conditions, and severity.
- Helps identify accident hotspots and safety trends.
![UK Road Accidents Dashboard](uk%20road%20accidents.png)
**Key finding:** Accident severity was noticeably worse in darkness compared to daylight despite fewer accidents occurring at night, pointing to visibility as a major risk factor worth targeting with lighting or awareness campaigns.
  
### 🛒 Real Mart Sales Dashboard
- Tracks sales, profit, customer ratings, and branch performance.
- Includes product-wise, city-wise, and monthly sales analysis.
![Real Mart Sales Dashboard](real%20mart%20sales.png)
**Key finding:** Sales varied significantly by branch and city, with the top-performing branch outperforming the lowest by more than 2x — a useful starting point for investigating what the top branch does differently.
  
### 🍕 Pizza Order Analysis Dashboard
- Analyzes pizza sales performance, order trends, category-wise sales, and customer purchasing patterns.
![Pizza Order Analysis Dashboard](pizza%20order%20analysis.png)
**Key finding:** Large-size pizzas drove the highest share of revenue despite not being the most frequently ordered size, indicating an opportunity to promote large-size upsells more aggressively.
  
### 🤖 AI Impact on Indian IT Sectors Dashboard
- Visualizes AI investment, employee growth, productivity improvements, and revenue growth across Indian IT companies.
- Provides insights into AI adoption levels and sector-wise impact.
![AI Impact Dashboard](AI%20impact%20in%20indian%20it%20sectors.png)
**Key finding:** Adoption levels were fairly evenly split across Low, Medium, High, and Very High categories, suggesting the industry hasn't converged on a dominant AI adoption pattern yet.
